**1. DeepMind's AI agents found two weaknesses in legacy RSA-1024** (@GoogleDeepMind) — Human-guided agents found a signature-forgery shortcut (~1,600× theoretical improvement for short fingerprints) and a batch-factoring method ~30× cheaper per extra key — but both target retired 1024-bit keys, 2048-bit and larger keys are unaffected, neither paper is peer-reviewed, and the factoring method has no implementation yet. *Why it's worth your time: AI-assisted cryptanalysis just got a real, measured result — and the "legacy keys still in old hardware" part is the actual risk to check.* [https://www.datastudios.org/post/google-deepmind-ai-agents-two-weaknesses-legacy-1024-bit-rsa](https://www.datastudios.org/post/google-deepmind-ai-agents-two-weaknesses-legacy-1024-bit-rsa)
